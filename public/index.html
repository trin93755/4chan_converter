Frame uses FFmpeg on your computer to make silent, size-checked videos for /pol/.

Getting started

  1. Add files or a folder. Your originals are always kept.
  2. Choose WebM or MP4. Optionally enable Catbox audio.
  3. Convert, then open the output folder or download a ZIP.

What “verified” means

Every output is decoded and checked: under 4,000,000 bytes, no more than 120 seconds, one VP9 or H.264 video stream, 8-bit color, and no audio. Videos are scaled to fit and long clips are split without discarding the rest.

The /pol/ board API reported a 4,194,304-byte limit and 120 seconds on October 13, 2026. Frame uses a smaller size target for headroom. Board rules and posting filters can change; a technical check cannot guarantee a post will be accepted.

Audio links and filenames

Base64url is the default: reversible and safe on every supported system. For Base64, decode %2F back to a slash first. Readable UTF-8 escapes : and / because operating systems don’t allow literal URLs in filenames. Percent and hex encodings are reversible too. Prefixes and suffixes sit outside the encoded link.

Each split part gets its matching audio clip. The output folder includes local MP3s and a conversion report with full audio URLs, even when a filename cannot hold a link. Filename encoding is not encryption. 4chan may shorten or rename displayed filenames.

No app signing required

Run Frame with Node.js 22+ and a full FFmpeg installation. It opens in your normal browser and uses a private loopback address. No installer certificate, account, or cloud conversion service is required.
