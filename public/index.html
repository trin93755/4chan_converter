Help

Add videos, pick WebM or MP4, and convert. Originals stay untouched.

/pol/ preset

Silent VP9 WebM or H.264 MP4. Under 4,000,000 bytes, up to 120 seconds, 8-bit color, and up to 30 fps. Every export is decoded and verified.

Long videos split into parts automatically. Choose Skip in Advanced to reject them instead.

Board limits checked October 13, 2026: 4,194,304 bytes and 120 seconds. Posting rules and filters can change.

Catbox & filenames

Catbox audio uploads each part’s soundtrack as a public MP3, saves a local copy, and puts its link in the filename. Anyone with the link can listen.

Advanced offers Base64url, Base64, readable UTF-8, percent, and hex encoding. Prefixes and suffixes sit outside the encoded link. Encoding is not encryption.

Base64 uses %2F for slashes; undo that before decoding. Readable UTF-8 escapes URL punctuation. Full URLs remain in conversion-report.json if a filename cannot hold them or 4chan shortens them.

Local app & privacy

4chan gem converter runs on your computer with Node.js 22+ and FFmpeg. Only optional Catbox audio goes online. No app signing or account is required.

Keep the launcher open while converting. Quit stops the app; completed exports remain in their folder. Use the pause button to stop Rin’s animations.
